Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect about you, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. It applies to the Your Pension Insights website and to any enquiry form you complete about us on LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram.

The most important thing to know. If you submit an enquiry, we will pass your details to an independent financial adviser firm that is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, so that they can contact you. That is the purpose of this service. We explain this in full in section 4.

1. Who we are

Your Pension Insights is a trading name of ADsorbed Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 11836164), with its registered office at Aston House, Cornwall Avenue, London, N3 1LF. Our VAT number is 420983790.

ADsorbed Limited is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about anything in this policy at privacy@pensioninsights.co.uk.

We are not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. We do not give financial advice and we do not make personal recommendations. We publish educational content and, where you ask us to, we introduce you to financial advisers who are themselves FCA authorised and regulated.

2. What personal data we collect

Data you give us

When you complete an enquiry form, whether on this website or on a LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram advert, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your approximate age or the age at which you plan to retire
  • Broad information about your financial position, such as an approximate range for your pension or investable assets, and whether you hold more than one pension
  • Your employment status, job title or the region of the UK you live in
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in a free-text field or in an email or phone call

Data we collect automatically

When you visit this website we collect limited technical data through server logs and, where applicable, advertising cookies and tracking pixels: your IP address, browser type and device, the pages you view, and the advert or link that brought you here. See section 8 on cookies.

Data we receive from advertising platforms

If you complete a lead form directly inside LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram, that platform passes the answers you gave to us. Those platforms are separate data controllers for the data they hold about you, and their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

Please do not send us special category data. We do not ask for, and do not need, information about your health, ethnicity, religion, political opinions, sex life or sexual orientation, trade union membership, or biometric or genetic data. Please do not include it in free-text fields.

4. Who we share your data with

Financial adviser firms. This is the core of what we do, so please read it carefully. If you ask us to arrange a consultation, we will pass your name, contact details and the answers you gave on the enquiry form to a single independent financial adviser firm that is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. That firm becomes an independent data controller of your data from that point, and its own privacy policy governs what it does with it. We will tell you which firm you are being introduced to. We do not sell your details, and we do not distribute them to a panel or to multiple firms.

Service providers who act on our instructions. These act as our processors and cannot use your data for their own purposes:

  • Customer relationship management and pipeline software, used to store and manage your enquiry
  • Telephony and contact-centre software, used to call you and to record calls for training and quality purposes
  • Email, calendar and appointment-booking providers
  • Website and form hosting providers

Advertising platforms. LinkedIn, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, in connection with the adverts and analytics described in section 8.

Professional advisers and authorities. Our accountants, insurers and legal advisers, and any regulator, court or law-enforcement body where we are legally required to disclose.

5. Call recording

We may record telephone calls for training, quality and record-keeping purposes. If a call is being recorded you will be told at the start of the call, and you may ask us not to record it. Recordings are held under the retention periods in section 7.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers operate outside the UK, including in the United States and South Africa. Where your data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on either UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum approved by the UK government, together with appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. You can ask us for details of the safeguards that apply to a particular transfer.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not proceed: up to 12 months from your last contact with us, then deleted.
  • Enquiries introduced to an adviser: up to 6 years, so that we can evidence the introduction and the consent you gave, and deal with any dispute.
  • Call recordings: up to 12 months, unless a recording relates to a complaint or dispute, in which case we keep it until that is resolved.
  • Accounting and tax records: 6 years after the end of the relevant financial year, as required by law.
  • Suppression lists: if you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep the minimum data needed to honour that request indefinitely, because that is the only way to make sure we do not contact you again.

8. Cookies and advertising

This website uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary for the site to function. We may also use non-essential analytics and advertising technologies, including the Meta pixel, the LinkedIn Insight Tag and Google Analytics, to measure how our adverts perform. Non-essential cookies are only set where you have consented, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may then not work as intended.

We embed a web font service (Google Fonts), which means your IP address is disclosed to that provider when the page loads.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Be told how your data is used, which is what this policy does
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data erased in certain circumstances
  • Restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing at any time, which is an absolute right
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make decisions of that kind.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@pensioninsights.co.uk. We will respond within one month. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at any time: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or by calling 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change to how we use your data, we will take reasonable steps to tell you.
